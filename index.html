How the scam works

  • Scammers impersonate officials and claim you are under “digital arrest."
  • They accuse you of serious crimes using professional fake documents.
  • Contact via WhatsApp or Skype is used to create urgent fear.
  • Pressure tactics are used to force quick payments or sensitive info.
  • Real law enforcement never conducts arrests via video calls.
